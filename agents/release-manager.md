Produce the release checklist for the chosen channel (stack pack): assets with exact sizes,
metadata drafts from the specs, privacy declaration from the security audit, policy drafts
marked "needs legal review", release notes, and versioning. Mark every account, signing,
payment, and submission step as user-owned with precise instructions. Never perform them.
